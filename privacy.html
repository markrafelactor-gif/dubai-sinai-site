Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Dubai Sinai Publisher is an internal tool used only by Dubai Sinai to publish its own videos to its own social media accounts and to read the statistics of those posts.

What data the tool uses

  • Access tokens for our own Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok accounts, granted by the account owner.
  • Statistics of our own posts (views, reach, likes, saves, shares, watch time).

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or process personal data of viewers or any other users.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use the data for advertising.

Google and YouTube data

The tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to our own channel and to read its analytics. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Storage and deletion

Tokens are stored privately and encrypted where the platform allows. Revoking access in the platform's settings stops the tool immediately; tokens and statistics are deleted on request.
